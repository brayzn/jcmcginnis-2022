A 4-hour workshop designed to help you reflect and refine the way you show up as a leader, the
culture you build, and ultimately help your team reach a new level of performance.

Inspired by remote leadership, but all applicable to in-person teams too. Expect to leave with a new
perspective on leadership and a set of tools to help you get there.
