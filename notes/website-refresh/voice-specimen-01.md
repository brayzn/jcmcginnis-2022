# Voice specimen 01 — Directed Chaos opening

Locked 20 October 2026. First author-approved passage. This is the benchmark for the refresh.

## Passage

My second day at GrowthLoop, I sat in on *sprint planning*. I remember thinking: oh good, they do this, this is a good sign. Then I saw the format. Every engineer. One meeting. Thirty fucking minutes.

The PM read through tickets. Not a discussion — a read-through. He explained decisions that had already been made somewhere else, in a meeting I wasn't in, and maybe you weren't either. After each one: “Is that okay?” In front of everyone. What were you going to say — no? With him ‘running the ship’?

My blood was boiling by the end. I decided pretty fast the meeting had to go. Is engineering just a fucking factory? That's a death bell for a company that wants to make decisions.

The worst part: he was trying to be helpful. He was doing what he thought a good PM does — keep everyone aligned, keep the tickets moving, make sure everyone was okay with it. And every time he asked “Is that okay?” he was teaching the room to say yes.

## What it establishes

- Detail before thesis.
- Polished and raw, close together.
- Rhythm: long sentence, short sentence, fragment.
- Judgment comes from the scene, not a goal.
- The villain is human; the helpfulness is the problem.
